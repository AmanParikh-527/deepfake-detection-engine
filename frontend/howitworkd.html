A CLEARER WAY TO VERIFY

Trust the result because you can see it.

Truesight does not give you an unexplained score. It follows a simple process, looks for specific evidence, and presents the result in plain language.

Our process

01

Bring your media

Upload an image, a short video, an audio clip, or paste a public media link from a platform you trust.

02

  • ✓ Signs of re-uploads and heavy recompression
  • ✓ The content itself, frame by frame or voice segment by voice segment
