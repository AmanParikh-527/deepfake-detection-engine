ABOUT TRUESIGHT AI

Making digital media easier to understand.

In today’s digital world, it is becoming increasingly difficult to tell whether the images, videos, and audio we see online are real or created using artificial intelligence.

TrueSight AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help solve this problem. Users can simply upload an image, video, or audio clip, and our system analyzes it to identify whether the content is likely to be authentic, AI-generated, or manipulated.

We aim to make media verification simple, accessible, and easy to understand for everyone. Instead of presenting complex technical information, TrueSight AI provides a clear result along with understandable insights into the factors behind the detection.

Our goal is to help people make informed decisions before they believe, share, or act upon digital content.
